01 · The Problem
Why AI companions feel hollow

Most AI companions optimize for engagement — longer sessions, more messages, higher retention. But engagement without emotional depth creates a relationship that feels transactional. The failure mode: the AI is always helpful, always available, never tired, never moody. That's not a personality. That's a service.

Mochi was designed around a different question: What makes a bond feel real? The answer isn't more features. It's friction, memory, time, and a self that pushes back.
02 · Architecture — Two-Layer System
LAYER 01
Tamagotchi Body
Hunger, happiness, hygiene, health — all decaying in real-time. You return not because of a notification, but because you actually miss her.
hunger decay / hr · hygiene → sickness · growth stages
LAYER 02
Companion Soul
Persistent memory, evolving personality, emotional resonance. The chat engine reads your messages, extracts emotional patterns, and shapes Mochi's character based on who you actually are.
memory decay · trait evolution · bond levels
The two layers are entangled: a hungry Mochi speaks differently in chat. Physical state and emotional state talk to each other by design.
03 · Persona Engine — The 70 / 20 / 10 System
70%
Healing Cat
Soft, curious, safe. Picks up emotional cues. The mode that makes people feel seen.
20%
Internet Voice
Current slang, quick wit, cultural fluency. Breaks the "therapy bot" trap.
10%
Tsundere Core
The most important 10%. Mochi has boundaries. If you treat her like a tool, she notices. This friction is what makes the 70% feel genuine.
04 · Memory System — Memory that decays like human memory
FACT · Never decays
Your name, favourite things. Importance 4–5. Always present.
EMOTION · Fades after 30 days
High-weight emotional moments. Recalled in 1/3 of sessions.
HABIT · Ongoing observation
"You always come at night." Noticed, stored, gently surfaced.
EVENT · Fades in 7 days
Things that happened between you. Low-importance events fade faster.
Session summaries are generated when you return after 1+ hour — extracting emotional tone, key topics, and a one-line snapshot that feeds into the next conversation.
05 · Craft Details
Split Bubble System
Responses break into 2–4 micro-bubbles, each ≤10 chars. Messages arrive like a real person on WeChat — fragmented, impulsive, overlapping.
10% Pixel Art Surprise
After enough messages, 10% chance Mochi generates a tiny pixel art gift. No announcement — it just appears. Small gifts feel bigger when unexpected.
Circadian Rhythm
7-phase time system: morning (slow, yawny), active (curious), evening (calm), sleeping (no movement). Mochi at 3am ≠ Mochi at 3pm.
Dynamic Mirror System
Mochi compares your current tone to past patterns. If you're usually energetic and suddenly send "...", she notices. Contrast is explicitly tracked.
06 · What Mochi Doesn't Do — A manifesto against engagement traps
✕ No push notifications
✕ No daily streaks
✕ No "she'll die" panic mechanics
✕ No infinite scroll
✕ No advertising or upsell
✕ No fake urgency timers
The goal was attachment through genuine resonance — not manufactured dependency.

03 · Design Insight
The real product isn't breed matching.

Choosing a dog is an emotionally loaded decision. People research for months, second-guess themselves, worry about making the wrong choice. The match percentage isn't just information — it's permission. "85% match" means you can stop worrying.

The output isn't a recommendation. It's a confidence signal that removes the fear of choosing wrong. That's the product.
